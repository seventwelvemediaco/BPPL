FAQ

Questions buyers ask us.

What does Bhagyoday Pumps make?

We manufacture reciprocating pumps under the UDAY brand: high-pressure triplex and quintuplex plunger pumps, metering (dosing) pumps and complete dosing systems, diaphragm pumps, fix-flow pumps and motorised hydraulic test pumps. We have been making them in Ahmedabad, India since 1991.

What is a triplex plunger pump?

A positive-displacement pump with three plungers driven by a crank shaft. It delivers a steady, high pressure (up to 700 kg/cm² / 686 bar in our range), which suits injection, jetting, RO feed, boiler feed and hydrostatic testing.

What is a metering (dosing) pump?

A pump that delivers an exact, adjustable volume of liquid, typically a chemical added to a process. On UDAY metering pumps you can set the flow anywhere from 0 to 100% while the pump is running, with accuracy within ±1%.

Which pump do I need for pressure testing?

For hydrostatic testing of vessels, pipes, castings and valves up to 120 kg/cm² (118 bar), choose the UFF-30 motorised hydraulic test pump. For higher test pressures, a triplex plunger pump is the right choice. Tell us your test pressure and volume.

Can the pump be made in stainless steel or special alloys?

Yes. Liquid ends are available in SS 304, SS 316, SS 316L and aluminium bronze as standard, and in Alloy 20, Monel or Hastelloy for special duties. Diaphragm heads are available for toxic or hazardous liquids.

Do you supply spares for older UDAY pumps?

Yes. Every UDAY part has a part code. Send us the pump model, or a photo of its name plate, and the part you need.

What information do you need for a quotation?

Liquid handled, flow rate, discharge pressure, suction conditions, operating temperature, material preference, and power supply (voltage and frequency). Our engineers will recommend the model and send a quotation.

I use bar or psi. How do I convert kg/cm²?

1 kg/cm² = 0.98 bar = 14.2 psi. For example, 100 kg/cm² ≈ 98 bar ≈ 1,422 psi. For flow: 1 m³/h = 1,000 l/h, and 1 US GPM ≈ 227 l/h. You can enter bar, psi, m³/h or GPM directly in the pump selector.

Do you accept enquiries from outside India?

Yes, we welcome enquiries from overseas buyers. Tell us your destination country, power supply and any inspection or documentation requirements, and we will reply with a technical offer.
