Triplex Metering

UDAY triplex metering pump.

Also called: multi-head dosing pump, multiplex metering pump.

Accurate dosing for corrosive chemicals, viscous liquids, oils, slurries, cryogenic liquids and high-temperature duties, with up to three heads on one drive.

Power end mechanism

Stroke from 0 to 100%. Running or stopped.

The power end turns high-speed rotary motion into slow, accurate reciprocating action. Plunger stroke length can be adjusted by hand while the pump is stationary or running, and flow is set by micrometric adjustment.

Liquid heads & valves

Wet ends made for your liquid.

Matched materials

Wet ends in standard or special materials, chosen for the physical and chemical properties of the liquid.

Interchangeable parts

Parts are interchangeable across metering duties for easy maintenance.

Accurate valves

Single and double ball valves, poppet valves with or without spring loading, and flat valves.

Special features

Compact. Robust. Accurate.

  • Complete, robust and light-weight with a wide capacity range
  • Totally enclosed, water- and dust-proof housing
  • Fully automatic lubrication
