Power end mechanism

Zero to full stroke, while it runs.

A precision-made high-speed worm drives a worm gear that rotates on an adjustable plane. Stroke length is set from zero to 100%, with the pump stopped or running, by micrometric knob or by pneumatic or electric actuator.

Diaphragm metering pumps

Hermetically sealed. Up to 385 kg/cm².

Hydraulically flexed diaphragm pumps for all gear sizes, from a few cc/hr to over 15,000 LPH. The plunger packing runs in clean hydraulic fluid, and a rugged diaphragm separates it from the metered liquid.

  • Hermetically sealed, with no leakage to atmosphere
  • Relief and replenishing valves in the hydraulic circuit
  • Better resistance to fouling
  • Long life from the PTFE diaphragm and gentle flow
  • Double and sandwich diaphragms with rupture detection
