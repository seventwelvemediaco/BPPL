High-pressure reciprocating plunger pumps.

Also called: high-pressure reciprocating pump, plunger pump, triplex / quintuplex pump.

Continuous-duty triplex and quintuplex plunger pumps with low maintenance, built to the rated horsepower and engineered for your liquid.

Overview

Up to 700 kg/cm².
Up to 1,907 l/min.

Six UT frames, UT-2500 to UT-20000, with plungers from 10 to 150 mm, so one family covers everything from chemical injection to bulk water disposal.

  • Chemical injection
  • Detergent slurry
  • Boiler feed
  • Process water disposal
  • Hydro-jetting & cutting
  • Vehicle wash
  • Hydrostatic testing
  • Reverse osmosis
  • Cement grouting
  • Salt water injection
  • Mechanical separation
  • Ammonia scrubber

Power end

Engineered for the rated horsepower.

Sealed crank case

Sturdy, completely sealed mild-steel crank case.

Alloy-steel crank shaft

Heavy-duty, accurately machined, hardened and ground.

Full piston-type cross head

Accurate plunger alignment with minimal side thrust.

Self-lubricated connecting rods

Accurately machined from high-grade material.

Pressure lubrication

Combined pressure lubrication to crank shaft and cross heads.

Tapered roller bearings

High-quality bearings support the crank shaft at both ends.

Liquid end

Crafted for the liquid you pump.

Liquid ends are made to order in corrosion- and fatigue-resistant materials. Diaphragm-type liquid ends are available for toxic and hazardous liquids.

  • Piston or plunger type liquid ends
  • Poppet, ball or disc valves to suit the application
  • Surface-treated plungers as standard. Ceramic-coated, hard-chrome or nitrided on request
  • Gland flushing, cooling and lubrication against specific orders
